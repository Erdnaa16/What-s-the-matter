1. The teacher divides the pupils into mixed- ability groups. 2. The teacher distributes a worksheet to each
group with guided sentences: • “Paul has a headache. He should ______.” • “You are going to get sunburn. You should
______.” 3. The teacher explains the task clearly: “Read the situation and complete the sentence
with the correct advice.” 4. The teacher reminds pupils to use the structure: • Subject + should + action
5. The pupils read each situation carefully. 6. The pupils discuss possible answers within
their groups. 7. The pupils decide on the most suitable advice. 8. The pupils write their answers using
should/shouldn’t. 9. The teacher walks around the classroom to
monitor pupils’ work. 10. The teacher provides guidance and support
to weaker pupils when needed. 11. The teacher reminds pupils to check their
spelling and sentence structure. 12. The pupils check their answers together with
the teacher. 13. Selected pupils read their completed
sentences aloud using full sentences. 14. The teacher provides feedback and corrects
any mistakes. 15. The teacher informs pupils that they will use
these sentences in the next activity.
